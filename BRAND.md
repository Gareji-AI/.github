# Gareji AI · Brand direction

## Core idea

A garage for building apps, tools, and games with AI.
Small projects and experiments that can grow beyond the place they started.

## Voice

English by default. Keep public copy short, plain, and modest.
Describe what we make. Avoid manifestos, grand claims, and repeated slogans.

## Profile description

A garage for building apps, tools, and games with AI.

## Visual direction

Charcoal, warm ivory, and vivid orange. An open garage with an upward diagonal accent evokes an idea taking flight. The banner expands the same idea into several active workshops.

### Banner prompt

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: wide landscape GitHub organization profile banner for Gareji AI.
Primary request: A panoramic editorial illustration of several humble home garages turned into inventive workshops, each making a different creation, with their ideas taking flight together into a vast open sky. Express energetic making from small beginnings, human and AI collaboration, many garages building for the world.
Style: sophisticated flat screenprint editorial illustration, restrained bold shapes, warm ivory and vivid safety orange against deep charcoal, subtle print grain, high contrast, architectural linework. Beautiful independent maker collective identity.
Composition: 3:1 wide panorama. Lower half: three varied small garage workshops, open doors, lit workbenches, a person sketching, a person assembling a small mechanical prototype, and a person working at a computer. Upper half: open charcoal sky with a few orange diagonal flight trails and simple paper-plane silhouettes rising from the workshops toward the right. Keep scene coherent and spacious, details readable at 850px wide. Asymmetry and strong directional energy.
Constraints: image only, NO text, NO lettering, NO wordmarks, NO typography, no watermarks, no existing company logos, no giant robot, no rocket, no globe icon. Use only charcoal, warm ivory and orange with muted warm gray. This is a real maker spirit, not corporate stock art.
